  • Drop first, sort laterEach vault opens on a chat with yourself. Drag in a file, then move it to Files, Links or Passwords.
  • Passwords stay sealedThey're encrypted on your device. A recovery code only you hold brings them to a new one.
  • Your storage, your limitVaults use your Drive's space, and deleting from Hive puts files in your Drive's trash.
